written: 29th July 2026

I'm gonna be honest, I think Sweden might be my favorite country in Europe. I've only beenthere a couple times in summer so this isn't a really good review, but still I want to write my opinions on it

  1. Its just a really beautiful place in general. Like theres a lot of forests and countryside which is REALLY nice. Lots of nature in general. So yeah things are a bit far from eachother, buuut it looks really nice.
  2. I like the houses there. The vast majority are just wooden and painted a nice red (maybe ill figure out how to add pictures) and a lot of the others are nice colors too.
  3. This point is really corny, but: kanelbulle. Its a type of cinnamon bun and its soo good. I legit gotta make those sometime.
  4. You can buy big blocks of cheese in the supermarket. Like 700g (or even more) of cheese. Then just take thin slices using an "osthivel" (idk how to spell it) and its really nice idk im just a #cheesegirlie :3
  5. The language is also really cool I think! I am kinda trying to learn it (insanely slowly, I started 4-5 years ago and i still cant understand much) and its pretty fun! idk why i like the language really but i think it sounds quite nice
  6. Also in summer there are so many blueberries its insane. You can just go in the woods and pick blueberries and its really fun. Yesterday I wanted to get a bunch of blueberries to eat and put in the muesli but i locked in wayyy too hard lmao

Theres more awesome stuff but this is what i can think of :)
